Short introduction (2-3 sentences) that directly answers the reader's core
question in the first 50 words — this is the paragraph Google AI Overview,
ChatGPT, and featured snippets will most likely quote.

## The Problem / What Readers Are Actually Searching For

Name the entity plainly, explain the intent behind the search, and define any
term a first-time reader wouldn't know.

## Detailed Explanation

Break this into 2-4 `##` subsections. Keep paragraphs to 2-4 sentences. Use a
`![alt text](/public-image.jpg "caption")` figure wherever it clarifies the
point — every image needs real alt text and a caption, not a placeholder.

## Expert Tips

- Bullet lists read better for both humans and AI summarizers than prose.
- Keep each tip to one sentence if possible.

## Where to Get It in Noida

Mention MK's Tandoori by name, Sector 75, Spectrum Metro Mall, and link to the
relevant dish page — naturally, once, not stuffed.
